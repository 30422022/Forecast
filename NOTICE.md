# Notice

This repository is a sanitized engineering preview. It contains no production model, model weights, training data, market-rule corpus, optimization strategy, or operational dataset. The optional PatchTST-style and FTMixer-inspired modules are independently written, randomly initialized teaching references and are not connected to the public API.

Names such as `ForecastProvider` describe extension points only. The included mock implementation is intentionally trivial and must not be presented as a forecasting result.

Research attribution: PatchTST, "A Time Series is Worth 64 Words" (https://github.com/yuqinie98/PatchTST); FTMixer, "Frequency and Time Domain Representations Fusion for Time Series Modeling" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15256). No upstream source files or pretrained weights are distributed here.
